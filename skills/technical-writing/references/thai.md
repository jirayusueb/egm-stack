# Thai technical prose

Apply with the standard layers when the reply language is Thai. Thai prose has
its own tells.

## Sentence structure

- One idea per sentence. Thai allows very long chains; split them at the first
  natural break.
- Put the condition before the instruction.
- Prefer the main verb early. A sentence whose verb arrives last loses the
  reader.

## Word choice

- Prefer the standard Thai word when one exists in a software context.
- Keep an established loanword rather than a forced coinage.
- Name code identifiers in backticks instead of transliterating them.

## Tone and filler

- No polite particles in artifacts. Conversation replies may keep them.
- Drop filler openers that carry no information. Start at the point.
- No promotional adjectives. State the fact.

## Formatting

- Keep spaces around inline code and English terms.
- Pick one quotation style per document.
- Numbers in Arabic numerals, including inside Thai text.

## Checklist

- Long sentences split.
- No information-free openers.
- Loanwords have no standard Thai replacement left.
- Identifiers sit in backticks.
- Artifacts are polite-particle free.
