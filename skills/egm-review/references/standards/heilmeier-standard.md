# Heilmeier Standard

A proposal passes when a reader outside the project can answer all eight questions without ambiguity. A question that does not apply is answered "not applicable" with a reason. A blank answer is a gap.

## The eight questions

1. **Objectives.** What is being attempted? Write it without jargon.
2. **State of the art.** How is it done today, and what limits current practice?
3. **New approach.** What is new in this approach, and why is it expected to succeed?
4. **Impact.** Who cares? If it succeeds, what difference will it make?
5. **Risks and payoffs.** What are the risks, and what is the payoff if it works?
6. **Cost.** What resources, compute, money, and headcount does it need?
7. **Timeline.** How long will it take, and what are the milestones?
8. **Metrics and exams.** What mid-term and final checks show success?

## Acceptance notes

- An objective with no measurable outcome fails question one.
- A state-of-the-art summary with no named limit fails question two.
- A new approach with no reason to expect success fails question three.
- A benefit with no beneficiary fails question four.
- A risk list with no payoff fails question five.
- A cost with no unit or period fails question six.
- A timeline with no milestone fails question seven.
- A success claim with no check fails question eight.
